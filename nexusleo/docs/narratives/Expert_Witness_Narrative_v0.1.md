## 1. Qualifications and Role

My role with respect to NexusLEO is as a technical expert responsible for the
design and implementation of the system’s analytic architecture. I am not a
sworn law-enforcement officer, prosecutor, or legal decision-maker. My testimony
is limited to explaining how the system operates, how it processes information,
and what safeguards are built into its design.

I do not testify to the truthfulness of investigative facts, nor do I testify to
the guilt, intent, or culpability of any individual. My testimony concerns the
system’s **process**, not investigative conclusions.

---

## 2. Purpose of NexusLEO

NexusLEO is an **AI-assisted investigative analytics platform** designed to help
law-enforcement personnel organize, review, and document information they have
lawfully collected during an investigation.

The purpose of NexusLEO is to reduce missed connections and analytic oversight by:

- structuring investigative data into discrete, evidence-linked claims
- preserving analytic reasoning over time
- surfacing ambiguity, contradiction, and gaps in information
- allowing investigators and supervisors to review how conclusions were formed

NexusLEO does not replace human judgment. It assists investigators by organizing
information in a transparent and auditable manner.

---

## 3. What NexusLEO Is Not

NexusLEO is **not**:

- a decision-making system
- a tool that determines probable cause
- a system that recommends arrests, charges, or searches
- a suspect prioritization or risk-scoring tool
- a predictive policing system
- an intelligence analysis system

NexusLEO does not decide what actions law enforcement should take. All decisions
remain the responsibility of sworn officers, supervisors, and prosecutors.

---

## 4. Data Ingestion and Source Integrity

When an officer inputs a report, interview transcript, or other investigative
document into NexusLEO, the system performs several integrity-preserving steps:

1. The source text is stored verbatim.
2. A cryptographic hash of the content is generated at ingestion.
3. The ingestion event is tied to an authenticated user identity.
4. The ingestion is recorded in an immutable audit record.

Once ingested, source materials are **not altered** by the system. This ensures
that all later analytic outputs can be traced back to the exact original content
submitted by the officer.

NexusLEO does not determine whether the content is factually correct. Responsibility
for factual accuracy remains with the submitting officer and the investigative
process, consistent with standard law-enforcement practice.

---

## 5. Claims and Evidence Linking

NexusLEO organizes analysis around **claims**.

A claim is a discrete, atomic assertion derived from investigative material, such
as a statement about a location, an association, or a time reference.

Every claim in NexusLEO is explicitly linked to evidence. Evidence consists of:

- exact text spans from source documents
- speaker attribution where available
- timestamps and document identifiers

If a claim cannot be traced to evidence, it does not exist in the system.

This design ensures that NexusLEO never produces unsupported conclusions. All
analytic outputs can be reviewed alongside the original supporting material.

---

## 6. Entity Representation and Ambiguity

NexusLEO represents people, places, and other investigative subjects as **entities**.
The system follows a conservative identity doctrine:

- entities are kept separate by default
- identity merges do not occur silently
- ambiguity is preserved and explicitly represented

When the system encounters a name or reference that could apply to multiple
individuals—such as a common name or incomplete identifier—it records multiple
candidate interpretations rather than selecting one.

These alternatives are presented to human reviewers for consideration. The system
does not assert that two references are the same individual unless there is clear,
documented support.

---

## 7. Confidence Assessment (ICD-203 Aligned)

Each claim generated by NexusLEO includes a **confidence assessment**.

Confidence reflects the **quality, quantity, and independence of supporting
evidence**, not the probability that a claim is true and not the likelihood of
criminal guilt.

NexusLEO uses a standardized, deterministic rubric aligned with analytic standards
commonly referred to as ICD-203. Confidence levels are:

- **High (Green):** supported by multiple independent sources
- **Moderate (Yellow):** supported by limited evidence without contradiction
- **Low (Red):** supported by weak, ambiguous, or conflicting evidence
- **Undetermined (Grey):** insufficient information to assess

The rubric used to assign confidence is versioned and recorded. The same inputs
will always produce the same confidence level.

---

## 8. Auditability and Reproducibility

NexusLEO is designed to “show its work.”

Every analytic step—ingestion, extraction, confidence assignment—is recorded in an
audit log that includes:

- who performed the action
- what action occurred
- which tool and version were used
- what inputs were consumed
- what outputs were produced
- when the action occurred

These audit records allow an independent reviewer to reconstruct how a particular
analytic output was produced, using the same data and the same rules.

---

## 9. Human Oversight and Decision Authority

At all times, NexusLEO operates under human oversight.

The system does not act independently and does not make enforcement decisions.
Investigators and supervisors review claims, confidence levels, and evidence
directly. Prosecutors evaluate admissibility and relevance independently.

NexusLEO’s role is to assist humans by organizing information—not to replace human
judgment or authority.

---

## 10. Summary for the Court

In summary:

- NexusLEO is an analytic assistance tool, not a decision-maker.
- All outputs are evidence-linked and auditable.
- Confidence assessments describe evidentiary support, not guilt or likelihood.
- Original investigative materials remain intact and traceable.
- Human officers and prosecutors retain full authority and responsibility.

My testimony is limited to explaining how the system operates and how its safeguards
function. Any investigative conclusions drawn from NexusLEO outputs are made by
human decision-makers, not by the system itself.
