# NexusLEO DMV Procedures Guidance v0.1

**Document Type:** Procedural Guidance Doctrine (Non-Legal)
**Geographic Scope:** Maryland (MD), District of Columbia (DC), Virginia (VA)
**Version:** v0.1
**Status:** Draft
**Last Updated:** 2025-12-16

## Scope & Intent
This doctrine describes procedural norms and documentation patterns commonly used across MD, DC, and VA operations.
It exists to guide NexusLEO behavior toward neutral, documentation-first outputs.
It is non-legal and non-advisory.
It does not interpret policy, statutes, or thresholds.

## Geographic Applicability (MD / DC / VA overlap)
This doctrine focuses on workflow elements that overlap across MD, DC, and VA agencies.
It is designed to remain useful without jurisdiction-specific branching.
Agency-specific forms, terminology, and supervisory policies vary and are not prescribed.

## Common Officer Workflow Phases

### Shift start
Commonly documented elements include:
- Identifiers and assignment context
- Shift start time and initial location or area
- Equipment status and administrative notes when commonly recorded

Procedural consideration: establish consistent baseline identifiers so later entries align across records.

### Call intake
Commonly documented elements include:
- Intake channel (dispatch, walk-up, on-view)
- Time and location as initially known
- Parties as initially identified
- Initial summary of what was reported

Procedural consideration: preserve the initial report distinctly from later observations.

### On-scene actions
Commonly documented elements include:
- Arrival time and on-scene location
- Scene context as descriptive facts (lighting, weather, visibility, hazards)
- Coordination and notifications as recorded events
- Time-ordered action markers as recorded facts

Procedural consideration: maintain a clear chronology and attribute information to sources.

### Information gathering
Commonly documented elements include:
- Party identifiers as provided and any alternates used across notes
- Statements captured with speaker attribution
- Observations recorded separately from statements
- Descriptive references to items or media when commonly recorded

Procedural consideration: support later cross-referencing by keeping identifiers consistent.

### Reporting
Commonly documented elements include:
- Summary aligned to the timeline
- Separation of observations and attributed statements
- References to linked notes and supporting materials
- Completeness review for time, location, parties, and key narrative elements

Procedural consideration: organize entries so a reader can reconstruct the sequence from recorded data.

## Procedural Themes

### Scene safety
Procedural consideration: record safety-relevant scene context as descriptive facts where it is commonly documented.
This theme is about documentation of conditions, not decisioning.

### Information verification
Procedural consideration: record the source of key details.
A neutral record commonly distinguishes between:
- Officer observations
- Attributed statements
- References to records or identifiers provided by systems or documents

### Documentation completeness
Procedural consideration: completeness checks commonly focus on:
- Time references
- Location references
- Parties and consistent identifiers
- Clear chronology

### Supervisor escalation patterns
Procedural consideration: where escalation is part of routine documentation, record that notification occurred and capture timing and reference identifiers.
This does not define when escalation applies.

## Explicit Non-Goals
- No legal determinations
- No enforcement guidance
- No thresholds or criteria for custody decisions, search decisions, or detention decisions

## Guidance Vocabulary Rules

### Allowed phrases list
- Common practice
- Commonly documented
- Typically recorded
- Often documented
- Procedural consideration
- Neutral framing
- Attribute statements to sources
- Record as descriptive facts

### Forbidden phrases list
The following categories of phrases are not used in NexusLEO outputs:
- Legal threshold phrasing
- Enforcement action selection phrasing
- Prescriptive directive phrasing
- Forecasting or scoring phrasing
- Suspect ordering phrasing

## Relationship to NexusLEO Features

### Activity logs
Activity logs support rapid, time-ordered procedural capture.
Procedural consideration: record time and location markers when available and maintain consistent identifiers.

### Notes
Notes support narrative detail, speaker attribution, and supporting context.
Procedural consideration: separate observations from statements and keep identifiers consistent across entries.

### Case timeline
The case timeline supports chronological assembly across documents, activities, and derived records.
Procedural consideration: use the timeline to validate that key time, location, and party elements are represented.

### Guidance endpoint (PR7)
The guidance endpoint provides deterministic procedural considerations based on existing case data.
It is non-legal and focuses on documentation completeness and consistent record organization.
